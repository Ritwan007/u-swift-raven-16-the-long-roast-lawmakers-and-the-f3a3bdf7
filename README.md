## What it does

`main.py` writes a single satirical sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF:

> The lawmakers who will regulate the internet sent their own bill as a photograph of a printed email of a screenshot of a PDF.

The line was chosen from 15 scored candidates for its turn — the lawmakers' authority is set up in the opening clause, then undercut by the five-layer file they could not convert — and its punch landing on the final words.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
